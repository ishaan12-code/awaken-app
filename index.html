Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Awaken, or tap Manage subscription in Awaken's settings. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

How does the free trial work?

The yearly plan starts with a 7-day free trial. If you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, you won't be charged. Otherwise it renews at $39.99 per year. Awaken also reminds you before the trial ends.

Can I get a refund?

Payments go through Apple, so Apple handles refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, choose "Request a refund" and pick Awaken. If something went wrong, email us too and we'll help.

I got a new phone. How do I get Pro back?

Open Awaken, go to Settings → Restore purchases, using the same Apple ID.

Where is my video stored?

Only on your iPhone. It's never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes it.

Awaken didn't appear when I opened Instagram.

Check that Screen Time permission is on for Awaken (Settings → Screen Time), then reopen Awaken. If you use instant open, check the automation in the Shortcuts app: When Instagram is opened → Run Immediately → Awaken Check-in. If iOS asks to allow the shortcut, tap Always Allow.

How do I stop Awaken guarding an app?

In Awaken, go to Settings → Guarded apps → Change apps and deselect it. To remove instant open, delete the automation in the Shortcuts app. Deleting Awaken removes everything.
