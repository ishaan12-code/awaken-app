Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Awaken. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

I got a new phone. How do I get Pro back?

Open Awaken, go to Settings → Restore purchases, using the same Apple ID.

Where is my video stored?

Only on your iPhone. It's never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes it.

Awaken didn't appear when I opened Instagram.

Check that Screen Time permission is on for Awaken (Settings → Screen Time), then reopen Awaken.
