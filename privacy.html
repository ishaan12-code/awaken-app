AWAKEN

Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

The short version: everything you put into Awaken, including your video, goals, to-dos and check-in history, stays on your phone. We don't run ads, sell data, or track you across other apps. The only outside service involved is for handling your subscription.

1. Information stored only on your device

Awaken stores the following locally on your iPhone. It is never uploaded to us or anyone else:

  • The video message you record
  • Your goals and the reasons you give for them
  • Your daily to-do lists
  • Your check-in history (when you walked away or continued), streaks and settings
  • Your answer to how much time you spend on Instagram

We have no servers that hold this data and no way to access it.

2. Camera and microphone

Awaken asks for camera and microphone access only so you can record your video message. Recording happens only when you press record, and the video is saved on your device.

3. Screen Time

If you connect Awaken to Instagram, it uses Apple's Screen Time framework to show your check-in when you open the app. Apple's framework keeps your app usage information on your device; Awaken cannot see or send your browsing or app activity anywhere.

4. Subscriptions and purchases

Payments are processed by Apple. We never see your name, email, or payment details.

We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions. To confirm whether you have an active subscription, RevenueCat receives a random anonymous identifier created by the app, your purchase and subscription history, and basic device and app information (such as iOS version, app version, country and currency). This is used only to provide and restore your subscription.

5. What we don't do

  • No advertising and no ad networks
  • No selling or renting of data
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites
  • No third-party analytics

6. Sharing your win card

If you tap Share, Awaken creates an image and opens the iOS share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you choose where to send it. Your top goal only appears on the card if you turn that option on.

7. Deleting your data

Deleting the Awaken app removes all data stored on your device. To ask for subscription records held by RevenueCat to be deleted, email us at the address below. To cancel a subscription, go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone.

8. Children

Awaken is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
